# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Ask Annie

**Version:** 0.1 Pilot

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** KylloAI

---

# Purpose

Ask Annie is an AI-powered digital safety companion that helps people make safer digital decisions by analysing suspicious messages, emails, websites and other online interactions.

The first release focuses on simplicity, trust and accessibility.

---

# Problem Statement

Millions of people are exposed to scams every day.

Existing security products are often too technical, too complicated or only detect known threats.

People need an AI companion that explains risks clearly and helps them decide what to do next.

---

# Goal

Give users confidence before they:

- Click
- Reply
- Pay
- Share information

---

# Target Users

## Primary

Older adults

## Secondary

Anyone uncertain about digital safety

---

# Success Metrics

Pilot success will be measured by:

- Users understand the explanation
- Users feel more confident afterwards
- Analysis completes in under 10 seconds
- Positive user feedback

---

# MVP Scope

Users can:

✅ Upload a screenshot

✅ Upload a photo

✅ Paste text

Receive:

- Risk level: Lower Risk, Concerning, or High Risk
- Plain English explanation

Recommended next steps

---

# Out of Scope

No accounts

No payments

No chat history

No notifications

No social features

No mobile app

No browser extension

---

# Accessibility

Large buttons

Large fonts

High contrast

Simple language

Keyboard navigation

Screen-reader compatibility

---

# AI Principles

Always explain reasoning.

Never claim certainty.

Encourage users to verify.

Never shame users.

Prioritise user safety.

# Definition of Done

The MVP is complete when a user can:

1. Open Ask Annie.
2. Upload suspicious content.
3. Receive an AI assessment.
4. Understand the explanation.
5. Know what to do next.
