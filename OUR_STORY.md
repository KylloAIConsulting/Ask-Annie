## Our Story

Ask Annie began with a simple observation.

As AI becomes part of everyday life, millions of people are being asked to make decisions online that can have real financial and personal consequences. Every day, people receive suspicious text messages, emails, phone calls and social media messages, often without knowing who or what to trust.

While teaching AI confidence and digital skills, it became clear that many people didn't need another cybersecurity product—they needed a trusted companion that could explain things in plain English, without judgement or technical jargon.

Ask Annie was created to fill that gap.

Our goal isn't simply to detect scams. It's to give people the confidence to pause, ask questions and make informed decisions before they click, reply, pay or share personal information.

We believe technology should reduce anxiety, not increase it.

Every feature we build is guided by one question:

> **Will this help someone feel safer and more confident online?**

If the answer is yes, we're building the right product.
